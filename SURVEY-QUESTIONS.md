# Newton County Community Healthcare Needs Assessment

**Working survey instrument for TAN Healthcare | Review draft**

**Introduction:** Help shape future healthcare in Newton County. Tell us what healthcare you and your community need most. Your input will help guide conversations about potential outpatient services. No services are guaranteed. Completing the survey is not a request for medical care.

**Estimated time:** About 5–7 minutes (to be verified with participant testing)

## 1. Tell us a little about you

- **ZIP code (required):** Five digits.
- **Age group (required):** Under 18; 18–24; 25–34; 35–44; 45–54; 55–64; 65–74; 75+; prefer not to answer.
- **Gender (required response):** Woman; man; nonbinary; another gender; prefer not to answer.
- **Who are you answering for? (required, select all):** Myself; my child/children; someone I care for.

## 2. What healthcare do you and your family need? (select all)

Routine primary care/sick visits; annual checkups/screenings; pediatric care; immunizations; high blood pressure; diabetes; heart health/stroke prevention; stroke recovery/rehabilitation; women's/reproductive health; prenatal/postpartum care; menopause care; mental health counseling; substance-use/recovery support; long-term condition care; physical/occupational/speech therapy; dental; vision; other with free-text.

## 3. Which specialists would you like closer to home? (select all)

Cardiology; neurology; endocrinology; OB-GYN; dermatology; psychiatry; orthopedics; urology; pulmonology; gastroenterology; nephrology; rheumatology; pain management; pediatric specialists; other with free-text.

## 4. What makes it difficult to get healthcare? (select all)

Distance; lack of transportation; cost; no insurance; insurance limitations; appointment availability; lack of nearby specialists; inconvenient hours; prescription barriers; caregiving/childcare; physical accessibility; language/communication; internet/technology; no barriers; other.

Also asked (optional): Where do you usually receive care? How long do you typically travel one way? Have you delayed or skipped care in the past year due to cost, travel or access?

## 5. Imagine your ideal community clinic. (select all)

Evening/weekend hours; same-day appointments; walk-in sick visits; video specialist visits with an on-site healthcare professional; local laboratory, X-rays, or ultrasound; local prescription access; counseling; rehabilitation; dental; vision; affordable care/coverage assistance; transportation assistance; other.

Also asked (optional): Would you consider a video specialist visit from a local clinic? Yes, maybe, no, or unsure.

## 6. What should come first? (choose up to three; at least one)

Primary care; children's healthcare; specialists; mental/behavioral health; screenings/vaccines; chronic disease care; women's/pregnancy care; stroke recovery/rehab; labs; imaging; pharmacy; evening/weekend/same-day care; affordable care regardless of insurance; transportation/accessibility; dental/vision; other.

## 7. Anything else we should know?

- **Open response (optional):** “What would you like TAN Healthcare and its partners to understand about healthcare in Newton County?” 750-character limit, with a warning not to share private medical details.
- **How did you hear about this survey? (optional):** Facebook/social media; community event; TAN staff; senior center; community organization; friend/family; other.
- **How are you completing the survey? (optional):** On my own; with help from staff or volunteer.
- **Optional contact details:** Name, phone, email. If contact information is entered, a valid phone or email and explicit permission to be contacted about the project are required. Contact requests are saved separately from needs responses.

## 8. Review and submit

Answers are summarized for review, with a Back button to correct them. A spam-prevention check is required for a live submission. The form remains in **preview mode** without a secure server endpoint.

## Scope and reporting notes

- Outpatient-focused: emergency departments, overnight stays, and inpatient/hospital-level services are not included as clinic wish-list choices.
- Multi-select means responses can total more than 100% when summarized.
- ZIP, age group, gender and scope of answers permit breakdowns but can create small-group privacy risks.
- Volunteers and staff using tablets should tell residents that proposed services are under consideration, **not confirmed**.
- Findings from a voluntary public survey are not a random representative sample of all residents; outreach across age groups, geographies and community organizations is important.
